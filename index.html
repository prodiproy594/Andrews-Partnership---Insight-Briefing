Influence in the Age of AI: Four shifts shaping reputation leadership
We surveyed 171 senior reputation leaders worldwide. The findings reveal a profession navigating extraordinary disruption – and emerging from it with greater influence, sharper priorities and a stronger sense of what it takes to lead.
▪ Influence is growing: 70% of reputation leaders expect their influence to increase in 2026, with over 40% holding board roles.
▪ AI underleveraged: 91% of teams are already using AI, but only 16% apply it to crucial predictive risk modeling.
▪ Structural redesign: Over two-thirds of functions are structurally evolving, with rapid centralization leading the trends.
